Collect training samples

Pick a label, hold the sign steady in frame, and capture. Aim for 40–60 samples per static letter/number from a few angles and distances. J and Z are motion signs — use the motion capture mode instead of single frames.

Train the classifier

Trains a small neural network in your browser on the static-sign landmarks you collected. Motion signs (J, Z) don't need training — they're matched with DTW against your recorded templates directly.
